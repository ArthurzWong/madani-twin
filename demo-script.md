# MADANI Twin — Live Demo Script (< 60 seconds)

1. **[0–5s]** Open the app. The dashboard loads directly into the **Downside** scenario with the synthetic-data banner visible. Point out: Day-0 cash **RM50,000**, reserve **RM10,000**, earliest breach **Day 7**.
2. **[5–15s]** Click **Baseline** → "no breach, min RM25,000". Click **Intervention** → Day 7 = **RM15,000**, Day 30 = **RM25,000**, zero breaches. The chart shows all three trajectories with the reserve line and shaded breach window.
3. **[15–25s]** Open **Payment Guardian** → the breach alert names the responsible transactions (payroll RM25,000 + supplier RM20,000 on Day 7), the predicted date, cash and shortfall — plus delayed-receipt, duplicate and unusual-charge detections on other tabs' data.
4. **[25–40s]** Open **What-if Simulator** → choose *Delay a receipt*, set Day 9, apply → the full 30-day forecast, alerts and recommendations recalculate instantly. Then *Restore receipt to original* and *Reset scenario*.
5. **[40–50s]** Open **Forecast & Evaluation** → the live tables show **24/24 synthetic cases** and **31/31 acceptance checks** passing, with recall/FPR/lead-time metrics and explicit denominators.
6. **[50–60s]** Open **Data & Assumptions** → provenance labels, fixed dataset, method limitations, and the supplier-agreement checkbox (intervention stays conditional until recorded). Finish with **↺ Reset Demo** to prove reproducibility.
