## 1. Ekonomi MADANI: "Raising the Floor" — this product *is* a floor-raising tool

The benchmarks guide states the core mission: *"Raising the Ceiling of Malaysia's economic potential must ultimately serve to Raise the Floor of the rakyat's living standards. Both must rest on the firm foundation of good governance."*

**MADANI Twin's contribution:** MSMEs form *"the backbone of Malaysia's services sector, representing approximately 84.4% of all businesses"* (AI Nation 2030, citing SME Corp Malaysia 2025) and account for **39.7% of GDP in 2025** (Budget 2027). SME failure is a household income event. A tool that prevents avoidable cash-crises protects employment at the floor of the economy.

## 3. Quantified national targets the demo narrative maps to

- **13MP / Budget 2027:** raise **MSME contribution to 50% of GDP by 2030** (from 39.7% in 2025) through *"more productive and competitive domestic enterprises"*. Productivity-led survival of MSMEs is the denominator of that ratio.
- **13MP (Ch.2, MSME financing):** *"strengthening of productivity and competitiveness will be made as an important criteria in the provision of financing for MSMEs"*. → MADANI Twin's APPROVE/HOLD/REVIEW evidence trail mirrors the discipline lenders will apply; an SME that can *show* a 30-day projected trajectory and a documented intervention plan is demonstrating exactly the financial-management capability financing criteria reward.
- **AI Nation 2030 target I10:** *"Provide 1.5 million MSMEs with scalable access to AI"*; intended outcomes: *"Lower Adoption Barriers, Enhanced Productivity, Stronger Economic Resilience"*. → The product's optional-AI-but-deterministic-core design is the barrier-lowering pattern: automation of back-office *analysis* first.
- **AI Nation 2030 national targets:** up to **+1.2 pp incremental GDP growth attributable to AI** and **up to 300,000 AI-attributable jobs by 2030**; Malaysia among **Top 10 in Global AI Indices**. A trustworthy, explainable SME finance tool is a counter-example to black-box adoption and aligns with the plan's governance emphasis (e.g. emerging-technology governance targets for 30% of listed companies by 2028).
- **Budget 2027 SME financing plumbing (context for why cash-flow stress exists):** RM10 billion SJPP credit guarantees + RM10 billion BNM–CGC post-crisis guarantee scheme; RM5 billion SME Stabilisation Relief Facility (≈RM2.8 billion approved to >4,900 SME accounts as of Aug 2026) introduced for West Asia-conflict impacts; TUBE-S grants (486 youth entrepreneurs, RM7.3m matching grants); TUNAS-i soft loans (50 MSMEs, RM2.7m, ≤2% p.a.); Mekar Dinamik women-MSME financing (RM5m). Guarantees and relief facilities address *financing supply*; none of them fix *timing* inside the firm — the gap MADANI Twin addresses.
- **BNM 2025:** OPR cut 25 bps to **2.75%** (Jul 2025); GDP +5.2% (2025); headline inflation 1.4%; *"financing facilities and credit guarantees continued to offer targeted support for SME financing"*. Cheap-credit environments mask cash-flow fragility; rate normalisation or shocks expose it — early-warning tooling remains valuable in both regimes.

## 4. Macro backdrop for the demo story (all from the benchmarks guide)

2027 forecasts: real GDP +4.2–5.2% (RM1,909,833m, 2015 prices); fiscal deficit −3.3% of GDP (target ≤3.0%); CPI 1.8–2.8%; trade surplus +RM321,584m; unemployment 3.0%. The guide's own SME-relevant insight: productivity-led wage growth is required for MSMEs to raise wages *"while maintaining business competitiveness"* — which presumes the firm survives its cash cycles.
